Windows + Microsoft Excel 2016 or later (BarFlow BBS). Open the file and press Enable Content. To use them together, keep both files in the same folder: the Optimize button in BarFlow then opens Cutting Plan.

Cutting Plan is also available as a direct download: BarFlow_CuttingPlan.exe (Windows may ask you to confirm: choose More info → Run anyway).

What it does

  • Bar bending schedules per BS 8666 with real shape drawings
  • Automatic cutting length, total number and weight
  • Millimetres or feet-inches (lb), English or Arabic
  • Export the schedule to DXF for AutoCAD
  • Print to A4 with page numbers
  • Cutting list export to Excel

Free version

  • Shapes library, Settings and License windows
  • Sample codes 100, 201 and 500
  • Printing, DXF export and Optimize unlock with a license
  • Licenses are tied to one computer and have an expiry date

BarFlow Cutting Plan

  • A separate program that turns your bar list into a stock-purchase and cutting plan: which bars to buy, how to cut each one, waste and cost
  • Results in Excel: a plan per diameter, a summary and an order sheet
  • English or Arabic; 3 free trial runs, then a license (tied to one computer and to a number of runs)
  • Works alone, or together with BarFlow: press Optimize in BarFlow
