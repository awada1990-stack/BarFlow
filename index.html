BarFlow

Smart Bar Bending Schedules for Excel

Download BarFlow (free version) Request a license

Windows + Microsoft Excel 2016 or later. After downloading: unzip, open the file and press Enable Content.

What it does

  • Bar bending schedules per BS 8666 with real shape drawings
  • Automatic cutting length, total number and weight
  • Millimetres or feet-inches (lb), English or Arabic
  • Export the schedule to DXF for AutoCAD
  • Print to A4 with page numbers
  • Cutting list export to Excel

Free version

  • Shapes library, Settings and License windows
  • Sample codes 100, 201 and 500
  • Printing, DXF export and Optimize unlock with a license
  • Licenses are tied to one computer and have an expiry date
